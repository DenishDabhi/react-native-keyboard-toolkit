# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a welcoming and
respectful experience for everyone, regardless of background or identity.

## Expected behavior

Be considerate, communicate constructively, accept feedback graciously, and
focus on what is best for the community.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, and
publishing another person's private information without permission are not
acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainers using the contact
listed in the repository hosting settings. Maintainers will review reports
promptly and fairly, and may remove comments or restrict participation when
needed. Please do not include sensitive information in public reports.
